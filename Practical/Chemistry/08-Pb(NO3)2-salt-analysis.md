## Aim of the Experiment

To detect the acidic and basic radical of the supplied inorganic salt by performing dry tests and wet tests and then identify the salt.

## Physical Characteristics of the Salt

- **State:** crystalline
- **Color:** colorless
- **Odor:** odorless
- **Solubility:** odorless

## Dry Test for Acid Radical

Take a small amount of salt (about 0.5 g) in a dry test tube and perform the following tests.


| S. No. | Experiment                                        | Observation                                                                     |         Inference         |
| :------: | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------: |
|   1.   | Salt + dil.$H_2SO_4$ + $\Delta$                   | No characteristic gas                                                           | $CO_3^{2-}, S^{2-}$ absent |
|   2.   | Salt + conc.$H_2SO_4$ + $\Delta$                  | Reddish brown fumes after heating                                               |  $NO_3^-$ may be present  |
|   3.   | Salt +$Cu$-turnings + conc.  $H_2SO_4$ + $\Delta$ | Dense reddish brown fumes are produces and the rest of the test tube turns blue |  $NO_3^-$ may be present  |
|   4.   | Salt +$K_2Cr_2O_7$ + conc. $H_2SO_4$              | No reddish colored gas                                                          |        Cl^- absent        |

## Wet Test for Acid Radical

Take a little salt solution in a test tube and perform the following tests


| S. No. | Experiment                                                                                                                                                                    | Observation                                                      |            Inference            |
| :------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------: |
|   1.   | Salt solution +$AgNO_3$ solution                                                                                                                                              | No ppt.                                                          | $Cl^-, CO_3^-, SO_4^{2-}$ absent |
|   2.   | Salt solution +$BaCl^2$ solution                                                                                                                                              | No ppt.                                                          |    $CO_3^-, SO_4^{2-}$ absent    |
|   3.   | Salt solution +$NaOH$ solution + sodium nitroprusside solution                                                                                                                | No purple coloration of the solution                             |         $S^{2-}$ absent         |
|   4.   | Ring test: salt solution + freshly prepared$FeSO_4$ solution. Allowed the mixture to cool under water and then add 3-4 drops of conc. $H_2SO_4$ by the side of the test tube. | A brown  ring is formed between the junction of the two liquids. |        $NO_3^-$ confirmed        |

## Wet Test for Basic Radical

Prepare a small amount of salt solution in a test tube perform the following tests.


| S. No. | Experiment                                                                                    | Observation |          Inference          |
| :------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | :---------------------------: |
|   1.   | Salt solution + dil.$HCl$                                                                     | White ppt.  |       Group I absent       |
|   2.   | Above salt +$\Delta$ + $H_2$ gas                                                              | No ppt.     |       Group II absent       |
|   3.   | Boiled off$H_2S$ gas from the above solution + $NH_4Cl (s)$ + $NH_4OH$ solution till alkaline | No ppt.     |    Group III (A) absent    |
|   4.   | Above solution +$H_2S$ gas                                                                    | No ppt.     |    Group III (B) absent    |
|   5.   | Boiled of$H_2S$ from the above solution + $NH_4OH$ + $(NH_4)_2CO_3 (s)$                       | White ppt.  |      Group IV present      |
|   6.   | Above solution +$Na_2HPO_4$ solution                                                          | No ppt.     | Group V ($Mg^{2+}$) present |

### Group-I ppt. Analysis

The group-I ppt is filtered. To the ppt. water is added and then boiled. The ppt. gets dissolved. 


| Residue                  | Filtrate                     | Observation | Inference           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------- | --------------------- |
| No residue               | Divide into two parts.       |             |                     |
| $Ag^+, Hg_2^{2+}$ absent | (a) 1st part +$K_2CrO_4 (s)$ | Yellow ppt. | $Pb^{2+}$ confirmed |
|                          | (b) 2nd part +$KI (s)$       | Yellow ppt. | $Pb^{2+}$ confirmed |

## Result

From the given salt sample, it is found that the basic radical is $PB^{2+}$ and the acid radical is $NO_3^-$. Therefore, the given salt is $Pb(NO_3)_2$.
