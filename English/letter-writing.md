# Letter Writing 
- Types 
    1. Formal 
        1. **Official:** Job application, Leave application 
        2. **Business:** Quotation letter, Order letter, Complaints/Adjustments letter
        3. **Letter to the press:** letters written to the editor of a newspaper. 

## Parts of a Letter 
### For Formal 
1. **Heading:** sender's address, date 
2. **Inside address:** receiver's address, subject 
3. **Greeting/Salutation**
4. **Body:** divided into different paragraphs, minimum of three 
5. **Closing/Ending/Leavetaking:** name, signature/designation 
6. **Enclosures:** list of documents attached 

### For Informal 
1. **Date**
2. **Greeting/Salutation**
3. **Body**
4. **Closing/Ending/Leavetaking** 

## For Digital Letters 
### Font 
- **Text:** Times New Roman 
- **Size:** 12 
- **Spacing:** 1" - 1.5"
