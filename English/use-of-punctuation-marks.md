# Use of Punctuation Marks 
- **Punctuation Marks**: 
    1. Full stop (.)
    2. Comma (,)
    3. Question mark (?)
    4. Apostrophe (')
    5. Single quotation ('')
    6. Double quotation ("")
    7. Brackets [{()}]
    8. Semi colon (;)
    9. Colon (:)
    10. Exclamatory mark (!)
    11. Hyphen (-)
    12. Dash (_)
    13. Slash (/)

## Full Stop 
It is the longest pause in a sentence. It is used at the end of a assertive sentence/statement. Also used with abbreviations, degree, title. 

## Comma 
It is the shortest pause in a sentence, used to separate words belonging to the same part of speech. Also at the end of salutation and leavetaking in letter writing. 
n
## Semicolon 
It is a pause longer than a comma but shorter than a full stop used to separate clauses and phrases or co-ordinate clauses. 

## Question Mark 
It is used at the end of interrogative sentences. 

## Exclamatory Mark 
It is used with exclamatory sentences. 

## Colon and Dash 
Used to introduce a quotation or a list. 

## Dash 
Also used for a sudden change in thought. 

## Hyphen 
Used for compound words, to separate cardinal numbers. 

## Apostrophe
Used for missing letters, possessive cases, to make numbers and alphabets plural ($3^s, R^s$) and with dates ($9'^{\text{th}}$) Oct 2025. 

## Quotation marks 
To introduce quotation and also for a quotation within a quotation. 

## Slash 
To give an option in Yes/No. 

## Brackets 
To give additional information, enclose year for additional information.
