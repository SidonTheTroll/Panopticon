# Subject-Verb Agreement
- Subject and verb agrees under 2 parameters: 
    1. Number 
    2. Person 

| Subject | Verb | 
|:-:|:-:|
| Singular | Singular | 
| Plural | Plural | 

| Subject | Verb | 
|:-:|:-:|
| 1st person | 1st person | 
| 2nd person | 2nd person |
| 3rd person | 3rd person |

E.g.: His knowledge of many languages **is** remarkable.  
E.g.: The results of his annual examination **were** bad. 

- **Subject word:** the most significant word in a many-word subject that determines the form of the verb. 

## Exceptions 
1. When two singular nouns are joined by 'and' and forms the subject, the verb must be **plural** but when the two singular noun refer to the same person, place, thing or object, the verb must be **singular**.
    - Gold and silver **are** precious metals. 
    - Bread and butter **is** his chief food. 
    - The poet and the novelist **are** dead.

2. Word joined to a singular subject using 'as well as', 'with', 'its', etc., the verb will be **singular**
    - Tea as well as coffee **is** grown in India. 
    - The cow with its calf **is** dead.
