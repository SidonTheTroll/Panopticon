# Narration 
> 15/10/25
- **Types**
    1. Direct speech 
    2. Indirect Speech 

- **Speech:** statement said by a person. 
- When reporting to a third person, we don't say the exact words but use indirect speech. 

> E.g.: He said, "I am very busy now."
> > He said that he was very busy then. 

- `said` is the reporting/principal verb. 

## Changes 
1. Before the indirect speech, use the conjunction 'that'.
2. Change the verb from 'am' to 'was'. 
3. Change the adverb from 'now' to 'then'.

## Rules 
1. If the reporting verb or principal verb is in past tense, then all the present tense of direct speech as changed to corresponding past tense in indirect speech. 
    - He said, "I am unwell."
        - He said that he was unwell. 
    - He said, "My master is writing letters."
        - He said that his master was writing letters. 
    - He said, "I have passed the exam."
        - He said that he had passed the exam.
    - He said, "the horse died in the night."
        - He said that the horse had died in the night. 

2. If the reporting verb or participle verb is in present tense, the tenses of the direct speech doesn't change. 
    - He says, "I am unwell."
        - He says that he is unwell. 
    - He says, "My master is writing letters."
        - He says that his master is writing letters. 

3. Pronouns are to be changed only when necessary. 
    - > Words expressing nearness in time and place in direct are changed to words expressing distance in the indirect. 
    - He said to me, "I don't believe you."
        - He said to me that he didn't believe me.
    - I said to you, "I don't believe you."
        - I said to you that I didn't believe him.

4. In reporting questions, the indirect speech must be introduced by verbs such as asked, questioned, enquired, wanted to know, etc. But the question is not introduced by an interrogative/question word. The verb must be followed by 'if' or 'whether'.
    - **Question words:** what, who, where, which, why, when. 
    - He said to me, "What are you doing?"
        - He asked me what I was doing. 
    - He asked, "Are you not Mohan?"
        - He asked if I am not Mohan.
