# Atomic Structures 
Scientists in the beginning of 19th century believed that matter is made up of very small indivisible particles called atoms.  
Later, as a result of large number of experiments they found that atom has complex structure and is made up of fundamental particles called electrons, protons and neutrons. 

- **Discovery of electron:** JJ Thompson by Cathode Ray Experiment. 

When high voltage is passed through the glass tube at very low pressure, it is sound that some invisible rays are emitted from the cathode, which is detected by the bright spot on fluorescent coating. These rays are termed as cathode ray. 

- Result of this experiment are: 
    1. Cathode rays travel in straight lines from cathode towards anode, in absence of electric or magnetic field. 
    2. When electric field is applied, the rays deflect towards positively charged plate. 
    3. When magnetic field is applied, they deflect in the direction expected for negative particles. 

Since atoms are neutral, therefore they must have an equal number of positively charged particles present in the atom to neutralize the negatively charged electrons. 

Goldstein discovered anode rays which traveled in the opposite direction to the cathode rays. 

- Anode rays are deflected by electric and magnetic field. They are attracted by negative plate in the electric field, i.e., anode rays are positively charged particles and was named **proton**. 

## Thompson Model of Atom 
According to Thompson, an atom possesses a spherical shape in which the positive charge is uniformly distributed and electrons are embedded like watermelon seeds. However, it couldn't explain experimental facts. 

## Rutherford's Atomic Model 
1. Most $\alpha$-particles passed through the foil undeflected; means most part of the atom is empty. 
2. A few positively charged $\alpha$-particles were deflected means considerable mass of atom must be present at small volume with positive charge. This positively charged central core where whole weight concentrated is called neutron. 

- **Atomic Number (n):** it is defined as the number of unit positive charges or protons present in nucleus of an atom. An atom is electrically neutral. $\text{no. of electrons} = \text{no. of proton = \text{Atomic Number}}$
- **Mass number (A):** the sum of number of protons and neutrons present in the nucleus of an atom is called mass number. 
    - $A = P + n$ or $A = Z + n$, [z = atomic number]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Chadwick in his experiment, bombarded Beryllium atom with $\alpha$-particles and observed that neutral particles were emitted. There were named as neutron. 

## Bohr's Atomic Number 
### Postulates
1. The electrons in an atom revolves around the nucleus only in certain circular path called orbits. Each orbit has definite amount of energy. $E_n = -313.6/n^2\ K\ \text{cal}/\text{mol}$
2. When the electron revolves, it neither absorbs nor emits energy. 
3. The energy is emitted or absorbed only when the electron jumps from one stationary state (orbit) to another. 
4. Only those orbits are allowed for circular motion of electron for which angular momentum $(mvr)$ of the electron is an integral multiple of $n/2\pi$, i.e., 
    - $mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi}$
        - $m$ = mass of electron 
        - $v$ = velocity of electron 
        - $r$ = radius 
        - $n$ = number of electron 
        - $h$ = Planck's constant = $6.626 \times 10^{-32}\ Js$

### Limitation of Bohr's Model 
1. Fails to explain spectra of multielectron atoms. 
2. Wave nature of electron is not explained. 

According to Bohr, electron is particle but DeBroglie proposed that it has wave character as well. 

## Modern Concept 
### Dual nature of matter (DeBroglie Hypothesis)
- DeBroglie proposed that matter has dual character, i.e., wave and particle. Since electron is also a matter, it also shows dual character, i.e., particle as well as wave. 

$$
\lambda = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{h}{P} [p = \text{momentum}]
$$

### Heisenberg's Uncertainity Principle 
- It is impossible to determine exactly both the position and momentum of an electron simultaneously. 
    - Mathematically, the product of uncertainity in position $(\Delta x)$ and uncertainity in momentum $(\Delta P)$ is approximately equal to $\frac{\lambda}{2\pi}$

$$
\Delta x \Delta P \approx \frac{h}{2\pi} \\ 
\because \Delta P = m \Delta V \\
\therefore \delta x \cdot m \Delta v \approx \frac{h}{2\pi}
$$

This principle has significance only in case of microscopic object.
