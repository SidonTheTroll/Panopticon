# Introduction 
Plumbing and sanitary systems are essential for maintaining hygiene and comfort in any building. Plumbing involves installing, repairing, and maintaining pipes and fittings that carry water, gas, and other fluids. It includes using accessories like taps and valves to control flow, and often requires cutting, bending, and joining pipes to fit specific layouts.

Sanitary work deals with collecting and disposing of wastewater through plumbing fixtures and sewerage systems. Together, these systems ensure proper water distribution and waste removal. A skilled plumber ensures safe, efficient, and leak-free installations that keep buildings functional and hygienic.

Therefore, it can be said that plumbing is the art and science of joining and installing various types of pipes and fittings required for water supply, sanitation and drainage systems. 

A plumbing system consists of pipes, fittings, and appliances used for water supply and drainage, including water, soil, sanitary, and rainwater systems. Using standard materials and ensuring good workmanship, supervision, and proper checks during installation helps maintain quality, efficiency, and durability.

# Tools and Equipments 
A plumber uses various tools for fitting, fixing, and repairs. These tools must be handled carefully, stored properly, and are classified as holding, fitting, cutting, threading, and bending tools.

## Bench Vice 
A bench vice is a tool fixed to a workbench and used to hold objects firmly during operations like filing, sawing, or threading. It has one fixed jaw and one movable jaw, operated by a handle and spindle to tighten or loosen the grip securely.

## Pipe Vice 
A pipe vice is used to hold pipes securely for assembly, disassembly, threading, or cutting. It comes in two types—open side and fixed side.

## Wrench 
Wrenches are hand tools used for tightening or loosening nuts and bolts, especially small or slippery ones. They are available in two main types—adjustable and non-adjustable—and are particularly useful for odd-sized nuts, bolts, and pipe fittings.

## Water-pump Pliers 
It is a common plier used by plumbers for holding, tightening and loosening work during fixing process. Steel is used for manufacturing water-pump pliers. 

## Pipe Wrench 
A pipe wrench is used to grip and turn pipes and fittings. Its adjustable, serrated jaws provide a strong hold for tightening or loosening threaded pipes.

## Pipe Cutter
A pipe cutter is a manual tool used to cut pipes easily at a work site. 

## Hacksaw Blade and Frame 
A hacksaw is a hand tool operated with both hands, used to cut materials like plastic pipes, steel rods, angle irons, sheets, and iron pipes. It can also cut jammed bolt heads and nuts. Its main parts are the handle, frame, blade, and adjusting wing nut.

## Pipe Bending Machine 
A pipe bending machine is used to bend or turn pipes. Its size and strength depend on the pipe’s diameter and material. 

## Threading Die 
Threading is an essential process for joining pipes and fittings securely. A threading die is used to cut threads on the pipe ends, allowing them to be connected with other pipes or fixtures.

## Chain Wrench 
Chain wrenches are used for holding and turning large diameter pipes where regular holding tools are ineffective. They consist of a toothed block, handle, and grooved chain that grips the pipe securely for tightening or loosening. Chain wrenches come in sizes from 3″ to 12″, corresponding to their maximum pipe-holding capacity.

## Measuring Tape 
It is used for measuring the length of an item. The measuring tape is manufactured in various materials like steel, plastic and PVC.
