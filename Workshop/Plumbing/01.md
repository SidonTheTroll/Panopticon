## What is Plumbing?
Plumbing is the art and science of installing and joining various types of pipes and fittings required for water supply, sanitation, and drainage systems.

## Requirements of Plumbing
Plumbing is required for transporting liquids or gases from one place to another through a system of pipes.

## Safety Precautions
There are three types of safety precautions in the workshop: personal safety precautions, general safety precautions, and workshop safety precautions.

### Personal Safety Precautions
1. Before entering the workshop, one should be alert and attentive.  
2. Never wear loose clothing.  
3. While working, avoid wearing a tie, muffler, wristwatch, or similar accessories.  
4. Avoid wearing shoes with nails or slippery soles.  
5. Always stay aware of your surroundings.  
6. Do not work in the workshop if you are mentally or physically unfit.  
7. Never touch electrical installations.  

### General Safety Precautions
1. Do not allow others to gather around you while you are working.  
2. Avoid moving aimlessly while operating a machine.  
3. Do not allow anyone to use any tool or machine without proper demonstration.  
4. Never work with damaged or blunt tools.  
5. Avoid talking to others while working.  

### Workshop Safety Precautions
1. Always wear goggles while working on a machine.  
2. Use the correct tools for each job.  
3. Keep every tool in its proper place after use.  
4. Do not disturb others while they are working.  
5. A first aid box should be readily available in the workshop and checked regularly.  
6. Inspect the machine properly before starting it.  
7. Keep your machine and workbench clean after completing your work and before leaving the workshop.

## Procedure 
1. Hold the tap in P.I. Vice and open the bush. 
2. Then unpack the stuffing box. 
3. Inspect all parts for wear. 
4. Clean the seat and other parts. 
5. Cut the washer with hollow punch and fit it. 
6. Then pack up the stuffing box with packing rope. 
7. Re-fit and test under water pressure. 

## Precautions 
1. Use screw spanner while fixing the tap. 
2. Do not use pipe wrench.
